## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, weighted equally:
- **Recruiters and hiring managers** screening for full-time, contract, or remote engineering roles. They skim fast, want role fit, real shipped work, and a CV.
- **Founders and businesses** looking to commission a system (automation, platform, app). They want proof the engineer ships complete, working products.

Both arrive from a CV link, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a direct share, usually on a laptop, often on a phone.
