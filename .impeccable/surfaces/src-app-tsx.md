# Surface: portfolio home (src/App.tsx)

Mode: Persuade (portfolio for recruiters and clients equally). Primary action: email. Secondary: download CV.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet, near-black, photo-led portfolio in the pinned fastfolio grammar, where the proof is production facts, not praise. Refuses the neon-glow "hacker terminal" look of the old site and refuses invented testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite ground #111 with raised #191919 panels, 1px #262626 hairlines, 24px card radius, full pill buttons (solid off-white primary with dark text and arrow; outlined secondary). Geist light display at large size, Geist body, Geist Mono only for dates and data. One live colour: a green status dot. Real brand icons in the stack cloud.

STORY: The visitor learns in one viewport who this is (full-stack, AI-integrated systems, Nigeria, remote), sees the face, sees shipped work with real screenshots, reads production facts sourced from the CV, scans the role history, then emails or takes the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top nav: avatar + name left; Skills, Work, Experience, Proof centre-right; solid "Contact me" pill right. Hero two columns inside a 1080px container: left a status pill "Available for work", H1 "Full-Stack Engineer." in Geist 300 at about 72px, a three-line intro, "See my work" outline pill + "Contact me" solid pill, then a drifting row of stack icon tiles. Right: the portrait in a tall rounded panel, same height as the left column.

FORM: Pinned by the owner (reference https://fastfolio.framer.website/); concept-seed roll not run because a user-pinned direction beats the roll. Signature interaction: the "Proof" marquee of production facts, plus a click-to-copy email in the closing banner. Motion grammar: one soft rise-and-unblur reveal per section, marquees paused on hover, reduced-motion respected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
