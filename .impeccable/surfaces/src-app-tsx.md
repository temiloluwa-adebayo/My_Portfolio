# Surface: portfolio home (src/App.tsx)

Mode: Persuade (recruiters and clients equally). Primary action: start a project / contact (amber). Secondary: see work, live sites, CV.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A cinematic, graphite portfolio where motion and real imagery carry the personality and one signal-amber colour marks every primary action. Refuses the flat, same-card grid and any invented social proof.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite #121212 ground, #1b1b1b panels, 1px hairlines, film grain at 4.5%. Clash Display 600 for headlines; Geist for UI and body; Geist Mono only for dates, hostnames, email. Amber #FFB224 with dark text only on primary buttons; secondary is an outlined pill; tertiary an underlined link. Green only for live/success states. Rounded 24–36px cards, soft offset shadows, 3D pointer tilt with sheen on desktop.

STORY: The visitor sees who this is and the headline over a crossfading, slowly zooming atmosphere slideshow; meets three featured systems as stacked cards with real screenshot carousels and a runnable LeadForge pipeline; scans five more; reads the stack by layer; picks a service; sees CV figures roll up; reads the timeline and FAQ; sends a message that actually delivers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating pill nav (avatar+name, section links with sliding pill, ⌘K search, amber "Hire me"). Hero: availability badge, role line, Clash headline "I build systems / that run themselves." revealed word by word in 3D, intro, amber "Start a project", outlined "See my work", underlined "Download CV", three proof bullets; right: tilting portrait card with name/location caption. Background: 4 Canva-generated atmosphere images at 55% under a left-weighted scrim, parallax on scroll, progress bars at bottom centre.

FORM: User-directed (redesign brief of 2026-09-25 with Watermelon UI components as references; Higgsfield unavailable, Canva used for imagery at the user's choice). Concept-seed roll not run: the user pinned the direction. Signature interactions: stacking featured cards on scroll, LeadForge "Run the pipeline" morphing button, ⌘K command palette, rolling digits, morphing contact submit, mobile bottom tab bar. Motion grammar: ease-out-expo reveals with a 10° rotateX that flattens; springs for anything interactive; everything collapses to fades under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
